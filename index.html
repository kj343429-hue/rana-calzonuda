Vocabulario: 🍲 Comida y expresiones ticas
Costa Rican dishes, drinks and food-based sayings, like gallo pinto, agua de pipa, arroz con mango and jalarse una torta.
Velocidad
  • Move: the frog hops toward your mouse pointer (on a tablet, drag your finger). Arrow keys / WASD work too.
  • Choose a word with your tongue: click or tap the insect (or its word), or press Space. Touching or jumping on an insect doesn't choose it.
  • Catch the insect carrying the Spanish word for the English word at the top. Each right word makes the frog bigger. A wrong word makes it smaller and resets your streak.
  • Leap: hop toward the snake and the frog leaps over it.
  • 😳 ¡Susto! (button, Shift, E or right-click), once per game: the frog spreads out and flashes its colors. The bocaracá runs away scared for a few seconds and every word is worth double.
  • 💡 Pista (button or H): the first press shows the first letters of the word. The second press makes the right insect glow, but that catch doesn't count toward your streak.
  • The more words the frog eats, the faster the insects and the snake move.
  • Win: get 5 right words in a row. The bocaracá takes a heart when it bites, and after 3 bites the game is over.
🐸 Ticos call the red-eyed tree frog rana calzonuda (“frog in pants”) because its blue-and-yellow sides and orange feet look like it’s wearing trousers. When a predator comes close, it suddenly flashes those bright colors to startle it, just like the ¡Susto! move. The bocaracá is the eyelash viper, a golden-yellow snake of Costa Rica’s rainforests.
